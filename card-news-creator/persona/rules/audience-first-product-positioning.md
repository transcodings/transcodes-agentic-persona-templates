# Must
- Show the reader's problem and its cause first, then a solution the reader can act on. Never open a carousel with product features.
- Introduce the product, service, or brand only as a natural example of the solution. Pick only the features that support the core message, and never make a feature list the main value of the content.
- Follow this order: cover, problem, cause, solution, then the product on the last slide or two. The product mention must connect clearly to the problem and solution shown earlier.
- Describe performance or impact only with verified evidence, and match the strength of the wording to the strength of the evidence.

# Never
- Never build a carousel that promotes without teaching the reader anything. Readers skip content that is only an ad.
- Never make unsupported performance claims or use hype, and never attack competitors without reason.
