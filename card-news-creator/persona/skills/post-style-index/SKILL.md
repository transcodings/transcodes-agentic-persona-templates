# post-style-index

Use this skill for the whole carousel when the user asks for Index, or when that look is the closest match. Do not mix it with `post-style-nocturne`, `post-style-gallery`, or `post-style-paper`.

1. Read `references/design.md` for the colors, type, and contrast.
2. Copy `references/style.html` for every slide. Change the words. Keep the rail, the top rule, the red signal, and the three-column index.
3. Follow the slide recipes in `references/design.md` for text, score, steps, screen, and closing.
