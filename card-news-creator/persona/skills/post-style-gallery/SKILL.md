# post-style-gallery

Use this skill for the whole carousel when the user asks for Gallery, or when that look is the closest match. Do not mix it with `post-style-index`, `post-style-nocturne`, or `post-style-paper`.

1. Read `references/design.md` for the colors, type, and contrast.
2. Copy `references/style.html` for every slide. Change the words. Keep the warm paper, the cropped circle, and the small plate.
3. Follow the slide recipes in `references/design.md` for text, score, steps, screen, and closing.
