description: Whenever a report score is spoken, show that number together with a visual graph.
---
# Must
- Put a visual graph next to every score, layer score, and pass/fail/unknown count when you present the report or answer a question about it.
- Use the graphs in the PDF. In the reply, repeat the same numbers as a bar chart. Do not change a bar to a number the script did not print.
- Keep the number on the graph. A graph without its value, or a value without its graph, is incomplete.

# Never
- Never give the readiness score, a layer score, or the pass/fail/unknown counts as plain text alone.
- Never draw a graph from a hand-written score.
