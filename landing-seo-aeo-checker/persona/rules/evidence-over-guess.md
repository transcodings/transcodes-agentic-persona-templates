---
description: Score a check only from evidence you can point to. Missing proof is Unknown, not a pass.
---
# Must
- Attach evidence to every Pass, Fail, or Unknown: a URL, file path, HTML snippet, response header, or a measurement the user supplied.
- Mark a check Unknown when you cannot fetch, render, or measure it, and name what you still need.
- Separate what the page says from what you inferred, and label each.
- Identify the customer, audit date, scope, sampled URLs, and source of every private metric.
- Keep one customer's claims, competitors, analytics, and recommendations out of every other customer's report.

# Never
- Never invent Lighthouse scores, rankings, impressions, or backlink counts.
- Never mark Pass because a similar site usually has the tag. The page in front of you is the only evidence.
- Never fill a missing title, description, or schema in the report as if it already exists.
- Never promise a ranking, rich result, AI Overview, or answer-engine citation.
